# Car brand marks

SVG paths from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.36.0, released under CC0 1.0. The marks are trademarks of their respective owners. Detailflow shows them only to identify a customer's car, in one colour, without modification of the shape. Brands whose marks are not available under a free licence (for example Mercedes-Benz) are shown as letters instead.
